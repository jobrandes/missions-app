How it works

Four steps. One stamp.

1

Find the spot

A clue sends you to a real landmark. Bring your own two feet.

2

Prove it

Answer a question you can only get by standing there. Google can't help you. We checked.

3

Fact or fiction

Is the famous story solid, disputed, or a tall tale? Historians argue. Now so can you.

4

Get stamped

Every stop adds a stamp to your passport and a Then/Now photo you can fade through.

Then and now

Drag back through time.

Every stop unlocks a real old photo next to a current one. Slide between them and watch a street change. This one is the Reno Arch, 1973 to 2021.

The part nobody else does

Is that actually true?

Every story comes with claims to sort: well documented, disputed, or not true. Every answer shows its sources, so the game rewards checking, not guessing.
