### Description (about 1,900 of 4,000 characters)

Go there. Prove it. Stamp the past.

Stamp the Past is a mission game for real places. Pick a town, find four landmarks, and watch the past fade into the present.

At each stop you:
• Find the spot from a clue and check in
• Answer a question about what is in front of you
• Slide between a real photo from the past and the same place today
• Play "fact or fiction" with the stories people tell about the place
• Earn a stamp in your passport

WHAT YOU GET
• 53 towns and 212 stops, from Reno and Virginia City to Boston, Chicago, New Orleans, Denver and more
• Real photos, old and new, every one from a free-to-use source and credited in the app
• Sources behind every story, and a plain label when a claim is disputed or not true
• A passport that fills up as you explore, with ranks, challenge seals and a share card
• Trails that cross towns: train stations, capitols, churches, bridges and more
• Works offline once it has loaded, so a patchy signal will not stop you at the steps of the courthouse

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. No location access. Your stamps live on your phone. A save code lets you move them yourself.

MADE BY A MOM AND HER TEENAGE SON
We wanted a reason to drag the family downtown. It worked.

Stories are written from published sources. We are walking the towns ourselves and mark each one until it has been checked on site.

Answer: **Yes, we collect data**, only through the optional report form.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")

No account or login is needed. Everything works with no setup. The app is a history scavenger hunt: open a town from the Towns tab, tap a stop, then "I am here" to check in (there is no location check, by design, so reviewers can try it anywhere), answer the question, and stamp the stop. The "Try it from home" button on the first screen lets anyone explore without being at a landmark. The app uses no location, camera, microphone or notifications. The optional "Something wrong?" form sends a short text report to our own server.
