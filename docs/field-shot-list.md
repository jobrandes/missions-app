# Field shot list (Jo-Anne verifies on site; Caden tests the app and sends ideas)

Photos you take yourselves are cleanly yours, so there are no license issues. Take them from public sidewalks, in daylight, with an adult. Do not step into the street, do not lean over railings, and keep people's faces out of frame where you can.

Landscape (wide) photos fit the app best. Hold the phone sideways.

## Most wanted
1. **The 2016 Virginia Street Bridge.** No free photo exists, so this is the biggest gap.
   - A wide shot from the sidewalk near the bridge's end, showing the bridge and the river.
   - A close shot of one of the old-style lamp posts. Four were restored from the 1905 bridge.
2. **The original 1926 arch on Lake Street.** A straight-on wide shot, and one from an angle.
3. **The Reno Arch.** One straight-on photo during daylight, and one at dusk with the lights on.

## Nice to have
4. Wingfield Park: the outdoor stage, the footbridge, and the island from the bank (stay back from the water).
5. A selfie-free "proof you were here" shot of the street sign at each stop, not for the app, just for your own camera roll.

## What I need from you
Attach the photos in chat (or drop them in a folder I can read), plus the date and place each was taken. I will resize them, add them with a credit to you, and update the rights log.

## Virginia City (all stops need photos)
Same rules. Wide, landscape, daylight, from the sidewalk. Bring water, the town is at about 6,100 feet. Stay out of saloons, casinos, old mine sites and fenced areas.
1. **C Street:** a wide shot down the street, and the street-name sign at a corner.
2. **Territorial Enterprise / Mark Twain Museum:** the building front from across the street.
3. **Piper's Opera House:** the front and the corner street signs.
4. **Fourth Ward School:** the whole building from the sidewalk so the steep roof shows.
For each, note the date and where you stood. Also one wide skyline-style shot of the town for the header.
