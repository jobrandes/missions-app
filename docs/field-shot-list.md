# Field shot list for Caden

Photos you take yourselves are cleanly yours, so there are no license issues. Take them from public sidewalks, in daylight, with an adult. Do not step into the street, do not lean over railings, and keep people's faces out of frame where you can.

Landscape (wide) photos fit the app best. Hold the phone sideways.

2. **The original 1926 arch on Lake Street.** A straight-on wide shot, and one from an angle.
3. **The Reno Arch.** One straight-on photo during daylight, and one at dusk with the lights on.
