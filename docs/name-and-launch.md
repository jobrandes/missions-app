## Before launch (Jo-Anne does these, Caden does not handle accounts or payments)
1. Pick the name, then check App Store search, USPTO, domain and social handles. Consider a short call with a trademark lawyer before spending money on branding.
2. Apple Developer account ($99/year), owned by an adult.
3. Public privacy page and support page. Drafts are in `site/privacy.html` and `site/support.html`. Add a real support email first, and host them on the final domain (the test site is marked noindex).
4. Wrap the web app as an iPhone app (Capacitor is the usual way). Needs a Mac with Xcode.
5. App Store privacy answers: "data not collected" is accurate today, but must be redone if photo memories or accounts are ever added.
6. Age rating questionnaire, screenshots (phone sizes), description, keywords.
7. In-app purchases for paid packs later, set up in App Store Connect by Jo-Anne.
