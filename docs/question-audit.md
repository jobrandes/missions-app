## Better question types (need an on-site check before shipping)
- **Count:** how many arched doorways, windows in a row, steps, columns.
- **Exact wording:** a short phrase on a plaque or sign that is not in the story text.
- **Color or material:** only when verified in person, and only for things unlikely to be repainted.
- **Direction:** "Which direction does the sign face?" (needs a compass check on site).

## Rules for the rewrite
- Jo-Anne confirms each answer in person before it ships. Nothing observed goes in unverified.
- Avoid answers that change (paint colors, banners, seasonal items).
- Keep the safety rule: no question requires going inside a business, crossing mid-block, or climbing anything.
- Keep a fallback hint that never gives away the answer.
